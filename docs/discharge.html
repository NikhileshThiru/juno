About Atrial Fibrillation

Atrial fibrillation (AFib) is an irregular heartbeat. It can cause blood clots that may lead to stroke. Your blood thinner (apixaban) lowers this risk. Your heart rate medicine (metoprolol) keeps your heart from beating too fast. Take both exactly as prescribed.
